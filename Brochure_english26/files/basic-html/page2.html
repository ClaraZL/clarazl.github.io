Chaco Province 2024 - 2025. Effective Large-Scale Reading Instruction
The Province of Chaco implemented a provincial literacy reform, replacing global approaches
First Grade
with the structured literacy program Aprendo Leyendo. To evaluate its impact, an international
team led by Lic. Florencia Salvarezza and composed of Haskins Laboratories, the Universidad de
la Ciudad de Buenos Aires, Yale Child Study Center, University of Connecticut, CONICET, and
Fundación INECO, conducted research with 1,319 1st and 2nd grade students from public schools.
The study compared the 2025 cohort, which received the intervention, with the 2024 control
cohort using the EGRA assessment.
Main Results
First Grade: The group that used the program significantly outperformed the control group across
all reading skills evaluated, achieving the most notable improvement in reading comprehension.
Second Grade: Students demonstrated significantly higher performance in letter-sound
knowledge (a fundamental skill), as well as in word reading, pseudowords, sentence reading,
and reading comprehension.
Conclusions
These findings support the effectiveness of the program in improving early reading skills during
the first years of schooling. Furthermore, the results demonstrate that structured and explicit
literacy instruction can be successfully implemented on a large scale.
